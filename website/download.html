  2. 2

Installation help

Why is the download not available?

An APK has not been configured as a verified public release. The site intentionally avoids showing a download link until a real release is published.

Android says it cannot install the app. What should I do?

Confirm that the file came from the official release source, that the download completed, and that your device supports the app. Do not bypass Android warnings for a file you cannot verify.

Does this app make real payments?

No. NUMPE is a demonstration project. Its sample wallet, balances, recipients, and payment activity are simulated and are not connected to a bank or UPI.
