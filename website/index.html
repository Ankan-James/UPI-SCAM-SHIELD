    Scam Shield

    More context. A better pause.

    NUMPE's Scam Shield experience demonstrates how text, links, or payment identifiers can be reviewed for familiar warning signs. The project is educational: it cannot confirm that a person, message, or payment is safe.

    • Review a request through a separate, trusted channel.
    • Never share a one-time password or payment PIN.
    • Use your payment app's own history to confirm a transfer.
    How Scam Shield is intended to work

    Remember

    A warning signal is not proof.

    Automated checks can miss scams or flag legitimate activity. Verify independently and use your bank or payment provider's official support channel when in doubt.
