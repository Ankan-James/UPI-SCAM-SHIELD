Digital payment safety

Take a breath before you tap.

Scams often rely on urgency, trust, or confusion. Slow the conversation down and verify the details through a channel you find independently.

Never share your payment PIN or OTP. A legitimate person helping you should not ask you to reveal either one.

Common warning signs

Know the pattern. Choose your next step.

These examples are educational. No checklist can identify every scam.

01

Fake payment confirmations

A screenshot or message can be edited, delayed, or unrelated to your account.

Do instead: Open your own official payment app and check its transaction history before handing over goods or services.

02

Suspicious IDs and requests

A request may come from an unfamiliar payment ID or use an unexpected refund, prize, or verification story.

Do instead: Verify the person's identity and payment ID using a known contact method before approving anything.

03

Manipulated QR codes

A QR code can lead to a payment or open a link you did not expect.

Do instead: Inspect the recipient and amount shown in your payment app before proceeding. A QR code is not a way to receive money by entering your PIN.

04

Fake customer support

An impersonator may contact you after you search for help and ask to install an app or share a code.

Do instead: Find support details in the official app or on the provider's verified website. Never share a PIN or OTP.

05

OTP and PIN theft

Someone may claim a code is needed to cancel, receive, or secure a payment.

Do instead: Keep one-time passwords and payment PINs private. Enter them only in the trusted app when you initiated the action.

06

Urgent payment pressure

Threats, secrecy, and countdowns are used to stop you from checking a request.

Do instead: Pause, contact the person using a saved number, and ask someone you trust if you feel pressured.

A quick safety check

Before you approve a request

  • 01Did I start this payment or request?
  • 02Does the recipient name and amount match what I expect?
  • 03Have I verified the request through a separate trusted channel?
  • 04Is anyone asking for my PIN, OTP, screen access, or secrecy?
  • 05Can I stop and check my official transaction history first?

If something feels wrong

Stop before sending money.

Do not continue the conversation through suspicious links or numbers. Contact your bank or payment provider using its official app or a number you independently verify.
