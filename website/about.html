About the project

Digital payment safety, made tangible.

NUMPE brings a simulated UPI-style app and a Scam Shield learning experience together so people can explore payment journeys without moving real money.

Demonstration project: NUMPE is not a bank or payment service and does not connect to UPI or any bank.

What NUMPE is for

The project explores how a payment interface can make room for a careful review before a user acts. It combines demo payment flows with explanations of common scam patterns and simple safety reminders.

The public website is informational. It does not create an account, process a payment, or submit content for analysis.

Mobile application

A simulated wallet experience

The Flutter app includes demo onboarding, virtual send and receive journeys, QR inspection, activity, scam reporting, and Scam Shield screens. Demo names, balances, QR payloads, and payments are simulated; no real transfer is initiated.

Backend

Separate API services

The Python FastAPI backend provides account, analysis, report, history, and other API routes. The app's current payment experience is a prototype and is not connected to bank or UPI infrastructure.

AI Scam Shield

Helpful signals, not a verdict

The project intends Scam Shield to help people notice suspicious payment signals. The current backend implementation uses rule-based text and URL checks and returns a risk score, indicators, and a recommendation; it is not a model-backed verdict. Treat results as educational signals only: they may be incomplete or incorrect and do not verify a recipient or guarantee that a transaction is safe.

Always verify payment details in the official app or service you use. Never share your PIN or one-time password, even if a screen or message appears reassuring.

What it does not do

No real-money transfers

  • It does not connect to a bank account or UPI network.
  • Demo transaction confirmations and balances are not evidence of a real payment.
  • Scam Shield does not guarantee fraud detection or replace your bank's guidance.
  • The public website does not depend on the backend to display its pages.
